# Direction

Experience mode. The scene leads and the controls sit under it.

Redesign of the earlier coastal wayfinding board. The old look was treated as evidence and an anti-reference. Seed 06ee51b0 assigned the phosphor terminal world; the product's own mechanism (a day you can drag through, a tide you can watch climb a beach) fused it into a lamp-room instrument: dark navy, lamp amber, sea glass, glowing numerals, a lit lighthouse beam at night.

Memorable moment: scrub through sunset and watch the sun go behind the dune, the lamp come on, and the water edge slide down the sand.
